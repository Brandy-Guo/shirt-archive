Website Introduction

This project begins with a white Maison Margiela shirt from the archive. From the outside, it looks simple. Look closer and you’ll find parallel rows of stitching on the cuffs, a pocket hidden inside and a diamond-quilted lining. These details tell us how the shirt was put together and how decisions about stitching, layers and pocket placement shape the finished garment.

We created this website to make our archive research available to more people. Click on different parts of the shirt to see close-up photographs and explore the details at your own pace. You can start with the label to learn about Margiela’s numbering system, follow the stitching around the cuff or look inside to see how the quilting gives the lining its raised texture.

Our printed book gives you another way to explore the same shirt. It brings short notes together with physical samples so you can feel the thickness of the layers and see how they move when you fold them. The website lets you look closely from anywhere, and the book brings that research into your hands.
